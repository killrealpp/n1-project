# Source Channel Style

`@num1_ch` is a high-frequency English market/news Telegram channel. It mostly publishes short factual signals rather than long commentary.

## Short Post Translation

Translate strictly into Russian market-news language. Keep source attribution and exact figures. Avoid expanding a one-line signal into analysis.

Preferred shape:

- one line when the source is one line;
- the same number of non-empty lines when the source is multi-line;
- same leading emojis or flags when the source starts with them;
- no added emojis, hashtags, numbers, links, or source names.

## Dzen Article Use

Use the channel as raw material for digests. A good article should group items by themes such as markets, crypto, Russia, China, energy, currencies, and companies.

Current article cadence:

- three channel articles per day in the current multi-channel setup: one for `russia`, one for `energy`, and one for `tech`;
- publish inside one configured daily window with a stable random minute per channel and slot;
- skip or manually force below the threshold instead of stretching thin source material.
